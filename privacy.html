Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

This site is a static publication. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not have a comment section or a contact form.

What is collected

Like nearly every website, the hosting provider records standard technical request logs (IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time). These logs are used to keep the site available and to understand which pages are read.

If advertising or analytics services are enabled on this site, those third parties may set cookies or read device identifiers in order to measure and personalise advertising. Where required by law, a consent notice is shown before any non-essential cookie is set, and you can decline it.

What is not collected

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers are requested or stored by this site.
  • No accounts, passwords, or payment details pass through this site.
  • No reading history is tied to an identity by the publisher.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the pages remain readable. You can also use your browser's private mode to avoid persistent cookies entirely.

Third-party links

Some notes link to manufacturers, public agencies, or shops. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
